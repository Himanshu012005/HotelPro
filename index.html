Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about booking and staying with HotelPro.

⏰ What are check-in and check-out times?

Standard check-in begins at 2:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Guaranteed early check-in or late check-out until 4:00 PM can be selected during booking.

🛡️ How does the Free Cancellation policy work?

All standard bookings include free cancellation up to 24 hours prior to check-in. If your plans change, you receive an instant 100% full refund with zero cancellation penalty.

☕ Is breakfast included in the room price?

Yes, our Executive Suites and Penthouse rates include daily hot buffet breakfast and barista coffee. For other rooms, you can easily add breakfast with a single click.

💳 What payment options are accepted?

HotelPro accepts all major Credit/Debit Cards, instant UPI QR payments, Net Banking, and offers a 'Pay at the Property' option upon physical arrival.
